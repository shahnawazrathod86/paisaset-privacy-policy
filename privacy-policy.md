# PaisaSet Privacy Notice

PaisaSet - Privacy and Data Protection
Updated: 22 October 2026 | Publisher: A3S Digitech
Contact: a3sdigitech@gmail.com

PaisaSet records manually entered income, expenses, notes, budgets, recurring bills and savings goals on your device. Notes may contain personal information; enter only what you need for budgeting. We do not require a user account or banking credentials. The app does not read SMS, contacts or location, initiate payments, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Storage and security: records are stored in a plaintext SQLite database inside the Android app sandbox. App Lock uses your device authentication; the app does not receive biometric templates or your device PIN. It does not encrypt the ledger. Android backup is disabled in the app configuration; manufacturer device-transfer behavior can vary. Keep your device secured.

Exports and restore: you can export CSV, PDF and plaintext JSON. JSON version 2 includes the ledger and budget settings with a corruption-detection checksum; it is not encrypted or protected against deliberate modification. Restore validates a complete backup and asks before replacing local records. Device-specific App Lock must be enabled again after restoration. Keep a current backup before replacing or removing records. Older incomplete JSON exports cannot be restored through this workflow.

Sharing: printing, opening a website, or selecting a share destination hands data to the app or service you choose. It may transmit or retain that data under its own policy. PaisaSet has no developer-operated financial-data server. Debug builds include network permission for development tooling; production permissions must be checked on the released artifact.

Recurring bills: bills are recorded as expenses only when you mark them paid. Optional daily local reminders can notify you about unpaid bills that are due; delivery depends on Android permission and power settings. Missed bills remain unpaid until you act. Reminders never automatically debit an account or create a payment.

Deletion and retention: Settings > Delete All My Data removes the local ledger and app-owned export cache. Copies you saved or shared outside PaisaSet remain at their destinations. App deletion removes app-private data subject to Android and device-transfer behavior. We do not promise forensic erasure of flash storage.

Privacy requests and questions: contact the publisher at the address above. No automatic claim of DPDP, COPPA or other legal certification is made. Applicable obligations and commencement dates depend on the law and actual processing. Do not store children's identifying details unnecessarily. Review the notice when updating the app.
